Important: This fan guide is an explainer, not a guaranteed-result calculator. The game has island-specific hidden recipes and chance-based breeding. Rare, Epic and Ruin pairings are not fully revealed here. For the actual breeding system, open the game and use its Breeding Structure.
Common breeding
Parents can combine their elements to match a common Shape available on that island. Parent Shapes can also be bred again.
Rare recipes
The game code describes Rare recipes as an island Quad plus a Triple, with a low chance.
Epic recipes
The game code describes Epic recipes as a Triple plus a Double, with a low chance.
Ruin recipe
Each island has a special pairing of two specific Triple Shapes for its Ruin Shape.
